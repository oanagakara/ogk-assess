OANAGAKARA (PTY) LTD – DATA SUBJECT CONSENT FORM

PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION ACT 4 OF 2013 (“POPIA”)

Consent to process personal information for learner placement assessment in terms of Section 18 of POPIA.

{# Section 1 #}

1. Storage, Retention and Destruction of Information

1.1 Personal Information is used to comply with legislation and to ensure that, in terms of the operational needs of OANAGAKARA (Pty) Ltd (Oanagakara), we have the information required to conduct and administer learner placement assessments. Information given will only be used for the purposes for which it is collected (specified hereunder), will be stored securely, and will be retained for a period of five years from the date of your assessment, after which it will be permanently and securely destroyed.

{# Section 2 #}

2. Reason why the Personal Information is required

2.1 Provided to enable assessment and placement:

  • Conduct the NQF Learner Placement Assessment and score your responses;
  • Generate a placement recommendation for submission to your institution;
  • Maintain assessment records for quality assurance, moderation, and audit purposes;
  • Provide statistical reporting on assessment outcomes to your institution or relevant accreditation bodies.

2.2 Required by applicable legislation:

  • Maintain records in accordance with the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA) Act, where applicable;
  • Report to relevant quality assurance bodies in terms of the Skills Development Act, where applicable.
{# Section 3 #}

3. Right to Object

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information. To do so, contact Oanagakara at info@oanagakara.co.za. A hold will be placed on processing while the objection is resolved.

{# Section 4 #}

4. Accuracy of Information

It is your responsibility to ensure that all personal information you supply is accurate, complete, and up to date. Please advise us of any corrections required.

{# Section 5 #}

5. Storage of Information

Your personal information is stored electronically in a secured database. Access is restricted to authorised Oanagakara assessors, moderators, your institution's appointed assessment administrators, and accredited auditors. Your information is not shared with any third party for commercial or marketing purposes.

{# Section 6 #}

6. Complaints

Complaints may be directed to Oanagakara at complaints@oanagakara.co.za, or to the Information Regulator of South Africa at enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

{# Section 7 #}

7. Access to Your Information

You may request access to your personal information at any time by contacting info@oanagakara.co.za to verify that the data held is accurate.

{# Section 8 #}

8. Declaration

I confirm that I have read and understood this notice and consent to Oanagakara (Pty) Ltd collecting, processing, retaining, and at the appropriate time destroying my personal information for the purposes of the NQF Learner Placement Assessment described above.

{# Honesty declaration #}

Assessment honesty declaration

I declare that this is my own work and I will not ask for help while completing this assessment.

By accepting below you confirm that you have read and understood the personal information notice above, and that you will complete this assessment entirely on your own without outside assistance.
