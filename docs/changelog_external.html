NQF Learner Placement Assessment Platform

Platform Changelog

Feature releases and improvements — assessor-facing changes

Authentication & Account Management

18 May 2026
  • New Assessors can reset a forgotten password via Forgot password? on the login page — a secure link is emailed and valid for 1 hour.
  • New Invite-based self-registration: any assessor can generate a single-use invite link from Invite Assessor in the sidebar and share it with a new colleague. The link expires after 48 hours.
  • New New assessors complete their own registration form — no administrator involvement required for standard assessor accounts.

Assessor Guide & Training Manual

9–18 May 2026
  • New In-app Assessor Guide accessible from the sidebar — covers sessions, attempts, marking, results, and working sheets.
  • New Assessor Training Manual (printable PDF, v1.4) — train-the-trainer format with a post-training sign-off checklist.

Consent Flow & Section Timers

3 May 2026
  • Improved POPIA consent modal unified across session and individual-attempt entry flows.
  • Improved Per-section timer: 45 minutes per section, up to 10 minutes review phase per section.
  • Fix Workstation number now visible in the consent modal.
  • Fix Learner redirected to instructions page (not questions) immediately after consent.

POPIA Consent Modal

26 April 2026
  • New Learners must scroll through and accept the POPIA notice before any assessment begins.
  • New Consent timestamp recorded on the attempt record.

Roles, Moderation & Results

25 April 2026
  • New Three access roles: Assessor, Moderator, and Auditor. Roles can be stacked.
  • New Finalisation workflow: assessor finalises a marked attempt; moderator can unlock if required.
  • Improved Results page: search by learner name or attempt code, date range filter.
  • New CSV export from Results page includes all scored attempts.

Score Audit Log, Font Picker & Error Handling

19–20 April 2026
  • New Score audit log: every score change (automated or manual) is recorded with who made it and when.
  • New Learner font picker: System, Comic Sans, Times New Roman, Arial, Century Gothic — preference persists across sessions.
  • Improved Custom error pages (400, 403, 404, 500) with clear messaging.

Working Sheets & Marking Interface

16–18 April 2026
  • New Print a working sheet for each learner from the session monitor.
  • New Upload a scanned working sheet to any attempt for record-keeping.
  • Improved Review queue scoped to written-response questions only (LIT-D-WRITE).
  • Improved Auto-marking fires on all other question types immediately on submission.

Security Hardening & White-Label Branding

19 April 2026
  • Improved OWASP compliance sprint: CSRF, session hardening, input sanitisation, rate limiting.
  • New Multi-tenant branding: organisation name, logo, and colour scheme configurable per deployment.

Session Management & Core Platform

13 April 2026
  • New Session codes: one code for multiple learners to join simultaneously.
  • New Session monitor: live grid showing every learner's status, progress, and time remaining.
  • New NQF placement logic: literacy and numeracy levels calculated from section scores.
  • New Results at a Glance: summary table of all marked attempts with placement outcomes.
  • New PostgreSQL database and Render cloud deployment.
