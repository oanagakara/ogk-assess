LIT-B-3 Max 2 marks   AUTO
Why does she want to join the learnership?

Memo answer: "She wants to support her family."

TierConditionMarks
Correct Response contains family AND at least one of: support · help · provide · care · look after 2
No match Any other response — including "family" alone 0
No partial mark for mentioning family without a motive. No review required.
LIT-B-4 Max 2 marks   REVIEW
In your own words, what is the main message of Maria's story?

Open-ended response. Always reviewed by assessor. No auto-mark criteria — assessor awards 0, 1, or 2 marks based on comprehension demonstrated.

LIT-C-1 Max 3 marks   AUTO
Why is this form important?

Memo answer: "The form collects basic personal information including full name, ID number, contact number, grade and disability status."

TierConditionMarks
Full answer Response contains ALL of: full name · ID number · contact · grade · disability 3
Partial — phrase Response contains basic personal information 2
Partial — keyword Response contains basic information 1
No match None of the above 0
Tiers checked in order — first matching tier wins. No review required.
LIT-C-2 Max 2 marks   AUTO
What does "if any" mean?

Memo answer: "It means that it exists."

TierConditionMarks
Full answer Response contains exist / exists / existing / existence AND response is ≥ 4 words 2
Partial Response contains exist / exists / existing / existence (any word count) 1
No match No form of "exist" detected 0
Tiers checked in order. No review required.
LIT-D-WRITE Max 8 marks   REVIEW MANUAL
Writing task: "Why I want to join a learnership and what skills I want to develop." (6–8 sentences)

Always reviewed by assessor. Scored on a rubric with criteria assessed by the assessor. No auto-mark criteria.

Blank Response Policy

Any question where the learner submitted no answer (empty or whitespace only) is automatically scored zero marks and does not enter the review queue, regardless of question type.
