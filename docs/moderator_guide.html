1. Role Overview

The platform has four user roles. This guide covers the two oversight roles: moderator and auditor.

RoleCan view results?Can view marks?Can unlock and re-mark?
Assessor Yes Yes (own work) No (once finalised)
Moderator Yes Yes (all attempts) Yes, on Full-mode templates
Auditor Yes Yes (read-only) No

What is finalisation?

When an assessor finishes marking an attempt, they click Finalise on the Summary screen. This locks the attempt: the assessor can no longer change any mark. The attempt then appears in the moderator's Moderation queue.

Moderation modes

Each assessment template has a Moderation mode, set by the platform administrator:

ModeWhat the moderator can do
Audit only Read all marks and responses. View the assessor's notes. Cannot unlock or change anything.
Full Read all marks and responses, then click Unlock to clear the lock and re-mark any question. Re-finalise when done.

If you are unsure which mode applies to the template you are moderating, check the Mode column on the Moderation page.

2. Logging In

Navigate to your platform URL in any modern desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended).

Enter your username and password, then click Sign in.

As a moderator you will see a Moderation link in the left navigation sidebar. Auditors see the same sidebar but without the Moderation link.

Important Do not share your login credentials. Moderator accounts carry elevated trust; they can change finalised marks on Full-mode templates. Contact your platform administrator if you need to reset your password.

3. The Moderation Page

Click Moderation in the left navigation to open the moderation queue. This page lists every finalised attempt, sorted with the most recently finalised at the top.

Each row shows:

  • Attempt code (click to open the full marking view)
  • Learner name
  • Assessment template and version
  • Mode badge: Full (green) or Audit (grey)
  • The assessor who finalised the attempt and the date
  • Action button: Unlock (Full mode) or View (Audit mode)
Badge count The number next to Moderation in the sidebar shows the total count of finalised attempts currently in the queue. The count does not decrease when you view an attempt; it decreases only if an attempt is unlocked (and therefore no longer finalised).

4. Reviewing a Finalised Attempt (Audit Mode)

In Audit only mode your role is to read and verify the assessor's work. You cannot change any mark.

4.1 Opening the attempt

Click the attempt code link, or click View in the action column. The marking view opens with a yellow locked banner at the top:

This attempt was finalised by [assessor name] on [date] and is locked.

All input fields and save buttons are disabled. You can read the question, the learner's response, the marks awarded, and the assessor's notes, but you cannot submit any changes.

4.2 Navigating questions

Use the sidebar on the left to navigate between questions. Questions that required manual marking are listed in the main sidebar; auto-marked questions appear in the collapsed Audit log section (staff users only).

4.3 Viewing the summary

Click Summary at the bottom of the sidebar to see a table of every question with awarded marks, maximum marks, and status. From the summary you can click Edit on any row to navigate to that question.

Working sheets If a working sheet was uploaded during marking, you can view it using the View button in the working sheet card at the top of the screen. This opens a modal viewer without leaving the marking view.

5. Unlocking and Re-Marking (Full Mode)

In Full mode you can unlock a finalised attempt, correct any mark, and re-finalise.

5.1 Unlock the attempt

Open the attempt from the Moderation page (click the code or click Unlock). The locked banner appears. Click the Unlock for re-marking button on the right side of the banner.

The lock is cleared immediately. The marking view reloads with all inputs enabled. The attempt is removed from the finalised state and the assessor's name is cleared from the lock record.

Notify the original assessor The platform does not automatically notify the assessor when you unlock their work. It is good practice to inform the assessor by other means (email or in person) before or after you make changes, so they are aware of the revision.

5.2 Review and re-mark

With the attempt unlocked, the marking view works exactly like it does for an assessor:

  • Use the sidebar to navigate to the question you want to change.
  • Enter the corrected mark (and update the assessor note if helpful).
  • Click Save or Save & Continue.
Auto-marked questions You can override an auto-mark by navigating to that question via the sidebar and entering a manual mark. The score audit log records both the original auto-mark and your manual override.

5.3 Re-finalise

When all corrections are complete, navigate to the Summary screen and click Finalise again. The attempt returns to the finalised state under your name and the current date.

The attempt reappears in the Moderation queue, now showing your name as the finaliser.

5.4 Score audit log

Every mark change is recorded in the score audit log. Staff users can view this log from the marking view (the Audit log link in the top-right area of the screen). The log shows: who made each change, when, the mode (auto or manual), and the points before and after.

6. Auditor Access

Auditors have read-only access to the assessor interface. The Moderation page is not available to auditors.

As an auditor you can:

  • View the Attempts list across all tabs (In Progress, Submitted, Marked, Incomplete)
  • Open any attempt in the marking view (read-only; inputs are disabled)
  • View working sheets
  • Browse the Results at a Glance page and export the CSV

As an auditor you cannot:

  • Create sessions or attempts
  • Mark or save any score
  • Finalise or unlock any attempt
  • Access the Dashboard
If you need to change a mark Contact your moderator (if the attempt has been finalised) or the assessor (if the attempt is still in the marking stage). Auditors do not have write access at any stage of the workflow.

7. Common Situations

SituationWhat to do
You cannot see the Moderation page Your account does not have the moderator role. Contact your platform administrator to have the moderator group added to your account.
The Unlock button is not visible on a finalised attempt The template uses Audit-only mode. Moderators cannot unlock audit-only attempts. If an unlock is genuinely required, contact your platform administrator to change the template's moderation mode.
You unlocked an attempt but want to leave it unlocked for the assessor to correct You can leave an attempt in the unlocked state. The assessor can then mark it and finalise it again. The attempt will not appear in the Moderation queue until it is finalised.
A mark looks wrong but you are not sure Open the working sheet (View button in the top strip) and the learner's response alongside the marking guidance note. If you are still uncertain, consult a colleague before making a change. All changes are logged and attributed to you.
The Moderation queue count does not decrease after you view an attempt Viewing does not remove an attempt from the queue. It remains finalised and visible until it is unlocked (which clears the finalised state) or the platform administrator removes it.
You need to see the original auto-mark for a question you overrode Staff users can access the score audit log from the marking view (top-right link). If you are not staff, ask your platform administrator to retrieve the audit log entry.
You accidentally unlocked the wrong attempt Navigate to the attempt's Summary screen and click Finalise immediately. This re-locks the attempt without changing any marks. Note the incident for your records.
