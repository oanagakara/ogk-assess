1. Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context indicates otherwise:

  • "Platform" means the NQF Learner Placement Assessment web application provided by the Provider, including all updates and enhancements made during the term.
  • "Site" means each satellite campus, branch, or physical location of the Client that accesses the Platform.
  • "Users" means assessors, administrators, and learners authorised by the Client to access the Platform.
  • "Assessment Data" means all learner responses, scores, attempt records, and associated data generated through use of the Platform.
  • "Enhancement Request" means a reasonable request by the Client to modify or extend the Platform's existing functionality.
  • "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or South African public holiday.

2. Services

2.1 Platform Access

The Provider grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to access and use the Platform for the Client's internal assessment purposes during the term of this Agreement.

2.2 Scope of Services

The monthly subscription fee covers:

  • Unlimited learner assessments and attempts on the licensed Sites;
  • Assessor and administrator access accounts (up to [__] named users per Site);
  • Platform availability monitoring and maintenance;
  • Reasonable Enhancement Requests as determined by the Provider;
  • Security patching and dependency updates;
  • CSV export of Assessment Data on request.

2.3 Excluded Services

The following are not included in the monthly fee and will be quoted separately:

  • Custom reporting dashboards or executive summary reports;
  • Bespoke integrations with third-party systems;
  • On-site training beyond the initial onboarding session;
  • Assessment template design or content development;
  • Major new feature development outside the existing product scope.

3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Monthly Subscription

The Client shall pay a monthly subscription of R 1,000.00 (one thousand rand) per Site, exclusive of VAT, invoiced on the first day of each calendar month and payable within 7 (seven) days of invoice.

3.2 Referral Discount

Where the Client directly introduces a new paying client to the Provider, and that new client activates a subscription of at least one Site, the Client shall receive a discount of 12% (twelve percent) on the Client's total monthly subscription for the duration of the new client's active subscription. The discount is applied from the month following activation and ceases if the referred client cancels.

3.3 Onboarding Fee

A once-off onboarding fee of R 8,500.00 (eight thousand five hundred rand), exclusive of VAT, is payable on signing of this Agreement. 50% is due on signature and 50% is due on completion of onboarding.

3.4 Late Payment

Invoices not settled within 14 (fourteen) days of the due date shall attract interest at the prime lending rate plus 2% per annum, calculated daily. The Provider reserves the right to suspend Platform access for accounts more than 30 (thirty) days in arrears.

3.5 Price Adjustments

The Provider may adjust subscription fees once per calendar year, with 60 (sixty) days written notice to the Client. Annual increases shall not exceed CPI plus 3%.

4. Service Level

4.1 Availability

The Provider will use reasonable efforts to ensure Platform availability of not less than 99% measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

4.2 Scheduled Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance windows are Sundays between 22:00 and 02:00 SAST. The Provider will provide 24 hours advance notice of any maintenance expected to exceed 30 minutes.

4.3 Incident Response

Critical incidents (Platform completely inaccessible) will receive a response within 4 Business Hours. Non-critical issues will be acknowledged within 1 Business Day and resolved within 5 Business Days.

4.4 Enhancement Requests

Reasonable Enhancement Requests will be scoped and delivered within a mutually agreed timeframe, typically 1–3 weeks. The Provider's determination of what constitutes a reasonable Enhancement Request is final.

5. Data and Privacy

5.1 Ownership

All Assessment Data remains the property of the Client. The Provider makes no claim to ownership of Assessment Data.

5.2 Data Processing

The Provider processes personal information (including learner names, ID numbers, and assessment records) solely for the purpose of providing the Platform services. Both parties agree to comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA) in respect of all personal information processed under this Agreement.

5.3 Data Export and Portability

On termination of this Agreement, the Provider will provide the Client with a full export of the Client's Assessment Data within 30 (thirty) days of the termination date, in CSV format, at no charge.

5.4 Data Retention

The Provider will retain Assessment Data for a period of 24 (twenty-four) months following termination, after which it will be securely deleted unless otherwise agreed in writing.

5.5 Security

The Provider will maintain reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect Assessment Data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. The Provider will notify the Client within 72 hours of becoming aware of any data breach affecting the Client's data.

6. Client Obligations

  1. The Client is responsible for managing assessor and administrator access credentials and ensuring only authorised personnel have access.
  2. The Client shall not share login credentials between multiple individuals.
  3. The Client shall use the Platform only for lawful purposes and in accordance with any usage guidelines issued by the Provider.
  4. The Client is responsible for ensuring learners are informed that their assessment data is being processed, in accordance with POPIA.
  5. The Client shall promptly notify the Provider of any suspected security breach or misuse of the Platform.

7. Intellectual Property

The Platform, its source code, design, and all derivative works remain the sole intellectual property of the Provider. Nothing in this Agreement transfers any intellectual property rights to the Client. Assessment content (question prompts, marking rubrics) uploaded by the Client to the Platform remains the intellectual property of the Client.

8. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Provider's total cumulative liability to the Client under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Client in the 3 (three) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, special, or consequential losses, including loss of profit or loss of data.

9. Term and Termination

9.1 Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues on a month-to-month basis unless terminated in accordance with this clause.

9.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 (thirty) days written notice. Fees paid in advance for the notice period are non-refundable.

9.3 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy that breach within 14 (fourteen) days of receiving written notice thereof, or if the other party is placed in liquidation, business rescue, or becomes insolvent.

9.4 Effect of Termination

On termination, the Client's access to the Platform will cease. The Provider will provide a data export as described in clause 5.3. All outstanding fees become immediately due and payable.

10. Dispute Resolution

The parties will attempt to resolve any dispute through good faith negotiation. If not resolved within 20 (twenty) Business Days, either party may refer the matter to mediation before a mutually agreed mediator. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, and the parties submit to the jurisdiction of the South Gauteng High Court.

11. General

  1. Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior representations, negotiations, and understandings.
  2. Amendments: No amendment to this Agreement is valid unless recorded in writing and signed by both parties.
  3. Notices: All formal notices must be in writing and delivered by email to the addresses recorded below, or by registered post to the addresses of the parties. Email notices are deemed received on the next Business Day.
  4. Non-waiver: Failure to enforce any right under this Agreement does not constitute a waiver of that right.
  5. Severability: If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
