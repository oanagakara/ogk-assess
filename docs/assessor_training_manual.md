## 1. Logging In

Navigate to **[https://your-platform-url.com]** in any modern desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended).

Enter your **username** and **password**, then click **Sign in**.

You will land on the **Assessor Dashboard**, which shows a summary of recent activity: attempts in progress, submitted, and awaiting marking.

> **Important:** Do not share your login credentials with learners or anyone who should not have assessor access. Each assessor should have their own account. Contact [your@email.com] to request additional accounts.

## 2. Before the Session

### 2.1 Create a Session

1. Click **Sessions** in the left navigation bar.
2. Click **New session**.
3. Select the **assessment template** from the dropdown.
4. Set the **seat limit** to match the number of learners expected in this sitting.
5. Click **Create session**.

A unique **session code** is generated (e.g., `AB12CD34`). This is what learners will enter to join.

> **Timing note:** Sessions expire 2 hours after creation. Do not create the session more than 15 minutes before learners are expected to arrive.

---

### 2.2 Print Working Sheets

Learners write their calculation working by hand on a **working sheet** during the exam. You print one per learner as they join.

**How to print:**

1. Open the **Session monitor** (Sessions → click your session → Monitor).
2. As each learner joins, their seat card appears.
3. Click **Print working sheet** on their seat card.
4. The sheet opens in a new browser tab. Print it immediately and hand it to the learner.

> **What's on the sheet:** The learner's name, their attempt code, and ruled working space for each numeracy question that requires shown working (NUM-B-2, NUM-B-3, NUM-C-1, NUM-C-3, NUM-D-2).

> **Important:** Collect the working sheet from the learner when they submit. You will scan or photograph it and upload it later during marking.

---

### 2.3 Share the Join URL and Session Code

Give learners the **platform URL** and the **session code**. They do not need a username or password; the session code is their entry point.

The join flow takes approximately 2 minutes:
1. Enter session code
2. Enter personal details (name, ID number, etc.)
3. Accept honesty declaration
4. Assessment begins

## 3. Running the Session

### 3.1 The Assessment Structure

Each assessment consists of two timed sections:

| Section | Content | Time limit |
|---|---|---|
| Section 1 | Literacy | 45 minutes |
| Section 2 | Numeracy | 45 minutes |

A countdown timer is visible to the learner at all times. When a section's time expires, the learner is **automatically moved to the next section**. Any unanswered questions in the expired section receive zero marks.

After both sections, learners may **review their answers** (90 seconds per question) or **submit immediately**.

---

### 3.2 Monitor Progress

The **Session monitor** shows a live grid of all seats. It refreshes every 30 seconds.

| Seat colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟡 Orange | Learner is in progress |
| 🟢 Green | Learner has submitted |
| 🔴 Red | Attempt was abandoned |
| ⬜ White (dashed) | Seat is empty / not yet joined |

Each card shows the learner's name, current question, a progress bar, and time elapsed.

> **Browser notifications:** The platform will ask permission to send browser notifications when you first open the session monitor. Allow this. You will receive a notification each time a learner submits, and a final notification when all seats have submitted.

---

### 3.3 Learner Cannot Continue

If a learner's computer crashes or they leave without submitting, their attempt will appear **Incomplete** (red) after the session expires. This is expected. Incomplete attempts can still be marked. If the learner needs to re-sit, create a new session.

## 4. After the Session: Marking and Finalisation

### 4.1 The Review Queue

After learners submit, navigate to **Review Queue** in the left navigation. This lists every attempt that still has questions requiring your input.

The badge number next to "Review Queue" shows how many attempts are pending. This number decreases as you mark.

Click **Mark →** next to an attempt to begin.

---

### 4.2 Upload the Working Sheet

Before marking numeracy questions, upload the learner's handwritten working sheet.

1. In the marking view, find the **Working sheet** card in the top strip.
2. Click **Upload**.
3. Select the scan or photo from your device. *(Accepted: JPEG, PNG, WEBP, PDF)*
4. The file uploads immediately.

Click **View →** at any time during marking to open the working sheet in a new tab alongside the digital responses.

> **Accepted file types:** JPEG, PNG, WEBP, PDF. A clear phone photograph is sufficient; no scanner required.

> **If the sheet was lost:** Score the working questions based on the digital answer only. Note "Working sheet not available" in the assessor note field.

---

### 4.3 Navigating Questions

The **sidebar** on the left of the marking view lists every question requiring your input:

- **Orange dot** = still needs marking
- **Green dot** = done (you can still click to revise)

Click any question to jump directly to it.

---

### 4.4 Scoring a Question

Each question shows three panels:

- **Left top:** The question prompt
- **Left bottom:** The learner's typed response
- **Right:** The scoring form

**If a rubric is shown:**
Enter marks per criterion. An optional note field is available per criterion for written feedback.

**If no rubric (manual mark):**
Enter a number in the "Points out of X" field. Use the assessor note field for written comments.

> **Marking guidance:** Many questions show a blue **Marking guidance** box with the expected answer or scoring criteria. Always read this before awarding marks.

---

### 4.5 Navigation Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Saves current question, stays on the page |
| **Save & Continue →** | Saves and moves to the next pending question |
| **Save & Review Summary** | Saves the last pending question and shows the summary |
| **Summary** *(top-left link)* | Jump to the summary screen at any time |

---

### 4.6 The Marking Summary

After all questions are scored, the **Summary** screen shows a table of every question with:

- Awarded marks
- Maximum marks
- Status: Done / Pending / Unscored

Use the **Edit** links to return to any question and correct a score.

#### Finalising an attempt

When you are satisfied with all marks, click **Finalise** (the green button at the bottom right of the Summary screen). Finalising locks the attempt: scores cannot be changed by an assessor after this point.

After finalising you are returned to the **Marked** tab in Attempts. The attempt moves out of the pending count and is available for moderator review.

> **Finalise is the point of no return for assessors.** After you click Finalise, you cannot change any mark. If you need to correct a score on a finalised attempt, contact your moderator or platform administrator.

> **Still have pending questions?** You can click Finalise even if some questions are still Pending. Verify you have no pending questions before clicking Finalise.

---

### 4.7 Auto-Marked Questions

The platform automatically scores many questions. Auto-marked scores appear:

- On the question's sidebar dot (green = auto-marked, no review needed)
- In the **auto-marked results** view, accessible from the attempt's row in the Attempts list

You do not need to re-enter auto-marked scores. If you disagree with an auto-mark, open the question in the sidebar and save a manual override.

## 5. Results and Reporting

### 5.1 Viewing Results

Navigate to **Results** in the left navigation. Results are shown per attempt with:

- Total marks awarded
- Total marks available
- Percentage score
- NQF placement recommendation

### 5.2 Exporting Data

Click **Export CSV** on the Results page to download a spreadsheet of all attempt scores. This file can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets.

## 6. Common Situations

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Learner cannot join: "invalid code" | Verify the session code is correct and the session has not expired (2-hour window from creation) |
| Learner's screen froze mid-attempt | Ask them to refresh the browser. Progress is saved per question. |
| Learner accidentally submitted early | The attempt is submitted and cannot be recalled. Note it and escalate to your line manager |
| Timer ran out before learner finished a section | This is expected behaviour. Unanswered questions receive zero marks automatically |
| Review Queue badge shows 0 but you have unmarked attempts | All auto-markable questions are done. Check the Attempts list for attempts without full scores |
| You scored a question incorrectly (not yet finalised) | Go to Attempts → find the attempt → click Mark → use the sidebar to navigate to the question → save the correct mark, then Finalise |
| You need to correct a mark after finalising | Contact your moderator or platform administrator. Only a moderator can unlock a finalised attempt. |
| Working sheet was not collected | Score the digital response only; note the absence in the assessor note |
| Learner disputes a score | Retrieve the attempt from the Attempts list and review the response and your assessor note |
